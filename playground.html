How the game works

Each round, both players secretly choose Cooperate or Defect. Their two choices combine into one of four outcomes:

3 · 3 both cooperate mutual reward 5 · 0 you defect, they cooperate, you exploit them 0 · 5 you cooperate, they defect, you get played 1 · 1 both defect mutual ruin

Cooperating together (3 · 3) beats both cheating (1 · 1) — yet the 5-point prize tempts each side to defect. A strategy is simply a fixed rule for when to cooperate or defect; the tournament reveals which rules win.
